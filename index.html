Current Commercialization Focus

Building air-leak detection.

Air leakage can increase heating and cooling loads, reduce comfort, and make building performance harder to diagnose. Glacia is developing tools to make leakage detection faster, more practical, and easier to deploy.

Core Technology

Locate unwanted airflow with greater speed and clarity.

Our focus is on practical building-envelope diagnostics that can help identify where uncontrolled air exchange occurs, support targeted remediation, and reduce wasted heating and cooling energy.

Why It Matters

Better air-leak detection can support energy retrofits, commissioning, weatherization, maintenance, and building-performance improvement.

Designed around practical deployment and real-world building diagnostics.

Noel Jensen

President & Chief Commercial Officer

Noel Jensen leads Glacia's commercialization and business-development activities, with a focus on moving early-stage engineering technologies toward real-world deployment. He holds a bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering and brings a strong technical foundation to customer discovery, partnerships, market development, and company operations.

Andy Feng, Ph.D.

Founder & Chief Scientific Officer

Andy Feng is a thermal scientist and engineer whose work spans heat transfer, advanced materials, and predictive physics-based modeling. He is an Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Washington and previously served as a tenured Associate Professor at the University of Utah. He earned his Ph.D. from Purdue University and worked as a Postdoctoral Fellow and R&D Associate Staff Scientist at Oak Ridge National Laboratory. His research and innovation have been recognized by honors including the NSF CAREER Award, the Brillouin Medal, an R&D 100 Award, the ASME Bergles-Rohsenow Young Investigator Award in Heat Transfer, and the Purdue Engineering 38 by 38 Award. At Glacia, he leads scientific strategy, technology development, and translation of thermal-science research into practical products.
